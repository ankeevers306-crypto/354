About 81dgital-ex

Founded in Bradford, England, 81dgital-ex is a trusted financial information consulting firm with over 15 years of industry experience. We have a professional team with rich experience in the financial industry, committed to delivering accurate market data, professional analysis reports and personalized consulting solutions tailored to each client's unique needs.

We adhere to the principles of integrity, professionalism and efficiency, and serve individual investors, small and medium enterprises and institutional clients across multiple regions. Our global perspective combined with local market expertise allows us to provide insights that drive informed decision-making and sustainable growth.

At 81dgital-ex, we believe that financial success is built on a foundation of clear information, strategic planning, and ongoing support. Our mission is to empower our clients with the knowledge and tools they need to achieve their financial goals in an ever-changing global economy.
